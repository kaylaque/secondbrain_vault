# Andrej Karpathy

## What it is

ML researcher, educator, and builder. Co-founder of OpenAI, former Director of AI at Tesla (Autopilot), creator of the "vibe coding" term, and originator of the [[LLM Wiki Pattern]].

## Why it matters to my work

Primary source for the LLM Wiki pattern this vault implements. His framing of knowledge management (wiki vs RAG, idea files, compounding knowledge) directly shaped the CLAUDE.md schema and vault workflows.

## Key contributions / outputs

- **LLM Wiki** — April 2026 GitHub gist describing the personal knowledge wiki architecture using LLM agents
- **Andrej Karpathy's Neural Networks: Zero to Hero** — YouTube series on building LLMs from scratch (nanoGPT, makemore, etc.)
- **micrograd, nanoGPT** — educational open-source implementations
- **Tesla Autopilot** — led the vision/perception stack; shifted from radar+lidar to camera-only
- **"Vibe coding"** — coined the term for LLM-assisted programming where intent > syntax
- **OpenAI co-founder** — left in 2023, returned briefly, now independent
