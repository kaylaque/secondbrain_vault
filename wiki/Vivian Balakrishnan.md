# Vivian Balakrishnan

## What it is

Singapore's Minister for Foreign Affairs (as of 2026). Retired eye surgeon turned politician. Notable as a non-technical government official who independently built a personal AI agent system for diplomatic work using open-source tools.

## Why it matters to my work

Real-world example of [[LLM Wiki Pattern]]-adjacent thinking applied by a non-engineer in high-stakes, knowledge-intensive work (diplomacy). Validates that personal AI knowledge systems are accessible beyond ML researchers.

## Key contributions / outputs

- Built a personal AI agent stack: Nano Explorer (containerized LLM interface via WhatsApp) + Whisper (speech-to-text) + Obsidian (knowledge base) + local LLMs via Ollama
- Keynote at AI Engineer Singapore (2026-05-16): "Building a Second Brain: Opportunities, Risks, and Implications for AI Adoption in Singapore"
- Three-message framework: (1) personal understanding/accountability cannot be outsourced, (2) real AI value at individual workflow level, (3) barriers to personal AI have collapsed
